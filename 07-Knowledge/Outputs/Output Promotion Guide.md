# Output Promotion Guide

## Promote only high-signal outcomes

After a published item has meaningful evidence of value, record one of the following in the appropriate knowledge page, research note, or AI memory page:

- A validated explanation or example that can be reused.
- A recurring audience question that becomes a research or knowledge question.
- A distribution or format pattern observed across more than one output.
- A correction that changes the evidence-backed working model.

## Do not promote

- Whole chat transcripts, draft fragments, vanity metrics without context, or unverified audience comments.
- A conclusion based on one reaction unless it is explicitly labelled as a single observation.

## Provenance

Link the published record in `05-Content/Published`, the relevant [[07-Knowledge/Indexes/Knowledge Index|knowledge page]], and any factual [[04-Research|research evidence]].
