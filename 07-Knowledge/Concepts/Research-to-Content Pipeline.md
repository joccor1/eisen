# Research-to-Content Pipeline

## Scope

Turning evidence and compiled knowledge into platform-specific original content without losing traceability.

## Working model

Start with a content brief that names the audience, claim, channel, evidence, and desired outcome. Use compiled concepts for framing and research notes for factual support. Only outputs that add a durable framework, result, or validated workflow are promoted back into the knowledge layer.
