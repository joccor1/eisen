# Source-Preserving Research

## Scope

Keeping external material intact while producing traceable, classified derivatives for navigation and analysis.

## Working model

Raw captures remain recoverable. A research derivative records the raw import, source metadata, classification, topic links, and the complete original content. Interpretation and synthesis belong in `07-Knowledge`, so source claims and the owner's reasoning remain distinguishable.
