# Knowledge Compilation

## Scope

Transforming source-preserving research into concise, linked, source-grounded concepts and syntheses that can be reused for decisions and content.

## Working model

The source and evidence layers are not replaced. Compilation creates a human-readable intermediate layer: concepts make stable ideas reusable, syntheses resolve cross-source questions, indexes guide retrieval, and a ledger makes reprocessing visible without making it mandatory.
