# Knowledge Base Operating Model

## Scope

The enduring structure and operating rules for Eisen's source-preserving, AI-native knowledge base.

## Synthesis

Eisen separates capture, evidence, compiled knowledge, and published output. A source can be captured quickly; a research note gives it one primary home plus cross-cutting topics; a knowledge page turns multiple evidence notes into a scoped and reviewable model; content then cites the relevant compiled knowledge and factual evidence.

The system compounds through deliberate maintenance rather than by storing every conversation. Durable agent outcomes are filed to [[97-AI-Memory/AI Memory Index]], active work is recorded in project status, and reusable knowledge belongs in `07-Knowledge`. The ingest ledger records history but never prevents re-ingest.

## Operating rules

- Preserve raw captures in `00-Inbox/Downloaded` and evidence in `04-Research`.
- Require linked evidence, confidence, scope, and a review date on concepts and syntheses.
- Use Topic Hubs and tags for cross-cutting navigation; folders define only the primary home.
- Feed back only high-signal content outcomes: validated examples, audience patterns, and reusable explanations.
- Treat lint results and review questions as work queues, not as automatic changes.
